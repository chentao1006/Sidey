A lightweight AI assistant for macOS menu bar and app context.

一款用于 macOS 菜单栏和应用上下文的轻量 AI 助手。

Sidey opens from the menu bar or as a temporary popover beside your cursor for the short AI jobs that appear while you work: explain a snippet, rewrite a paragraph, inspect selected text, or ask an assistant tuned for the current app.

旁白可从菜单栏打开，也可作为随光标出现的临时浮窗，处理工作中随手出现的小任务：解释一段内容、改写一句话、处理选中文本，或调用为当前应用准备好的专属助手。

Small surface, relevant answers

界面克制，回答贴近场景

  • The menu bar popover is always close, but never takes over your desktop.
  • 菜单栏浮窗触手可及，但不会接管你的桌面。
  • Different apps can have different assistants, prompts, and response styles.
  • 不同应用可以绑定不同助手、提示词和回答风格。
  • Use selected text, clipboard, and active-app context only when needed.
  • 按需使用选中文本、剪贴板和当前应用上下文。

Designed for short, app-specific work.

为短任务和应用场景而设计。

Sidey keeps the assistant close but not dominant. The useful part is not another chat app; it is the right prompt, in the right app, with the right context.

旁白不会把自己变成另一个沉重聊天窗口。关键是：在合适的应用里，用合适的助手，带上合适的上下文。

FAQ

常见问题

Is Sidey free? 旁白是免费的吗？

Sidey can be used with a limited public AI service. You can also configure your own AI provider for unrestricted usage based on that provider's terms.

旁白可使用带限制的公共 AI 服务。你也可以配置自己的 AI 服务商，实际用量以服务商规则为准。

Which AI providers are supported? 支持哪些 AI 服务商？

Sidey supports OpenAI-compatible APIs plus native formats for Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, and Ollama.

旁白支持 OpenAI 兼容接口，也支持 Anthropic Claude、Google Gemini 和 Ollama 的原生格式。

Why does Sidey ask for Accessibility permission? 为什么需要辅助功能权限？

Accessibility permission lets Sidey detect the active app and capture selected text for context. It is used for local interaction with your Mac.

辅助功能权限用于识别当前应用和捕获选中文本，帮助旁白理解上下文。该权限用于本地 Mac 交互。

让 AI 出现在小任务发生的地方。

安装旁白，为常用应用配置合适助手，然后在真正需要时再唤起它。
