Privacy Policy for Sidey

Last updated: March 2026

Sidey is a lightweight macOS utility designed to ensure selected applications fully terminate when their windows are closed.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Sidey handles your information.

1. Data Collection

Sidey does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No account system
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No personal identifiers collected
  • No usage data sent to any server

Sidey runs entirely on your local device.

2. Permissions

Sidey may require certain macOS system permissions (such as Accessibility access) to function properly.

  • Used only to monitor and control application behavior
  • Processed entirely on-device
  • Never transmitted externally

3. Data Storage

All configurations (such as selected applications) are stored locally on your device.

Sidey does not use any remote database or cloud storage.

4. Third-Party Services

Sidey does not integrate with third-party services, except for optional update mechanisms (e.g., Sparkle).

  • The app may check for updates from a remote feed
  • No personal data is included in update requests

5. Security

Since Sidey does not collect or transmit user data:

  • No risk of server-side data leakage
  • All operations remain local to your device

6. Children's Privacy

Sidey does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions, you can contact:

  • GitHub: https://github.com/chentao1006/Sidey
